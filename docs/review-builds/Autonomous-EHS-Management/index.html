What happens to a safety fix after the shift changes?

At North plant, maintenance has installed a guard after a conveyor near miss. The EHS team needs to know what workers saw on both shifts. Walk a related dock round, then review the conveyor records and follow the authorized reviewer’s decision into the technical guide.

The interactive workspace runs entirely in your browser. All records are synthetic and read only. There are no sign-in, uploads, submissions, approvals, or connections to a live database.

Self-hosted corporate EHS software

Autonomous EHS Management uses PostgreSQL as the system of record, with authentication, permission checks, and an audit trail. Optional AI can suggest wording. People review and close records. Module maturity varies.

The synthetic conveyor case runs in browser memory. It exercises no backend permissions, submits no records and makes no certification or filing determination.
