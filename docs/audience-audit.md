# Can visitors learn enough to make the next decision?

These cases follow the operating user from a specific incident to the evidence they need, the action they can take and the investment their sponsor would have to justify. The first tables below retain the business walkthroughs. The implementation table follows the visitor's selected case into each proposed technical view. This is an inspection of rendered software, fixtures and replay outcomes. Whether practitioners learn, enjoy exploring or save time requires a participant study. [Case screens, drafts and industry sources](demo-field-guide.md)

## Comparison with the APS case

APS starts with an assembly deadline, follows the buyer-led workflow, and lets a buyer change the release time, approval age and available stock. The technical view carries the same purchase into a request trace, offers two recorded research trials and identifies the local boundary tests. The business case asks whether measuring ordinary purchasing exceptions would justify an investment. [Purchase study](https://safetymp.github.io/Agentic-Process-Safety/#/study)

For the restored cases, the review asks whether a visitor can explore evidence before receiving an answer, see a consequence for someone else and take the selected facts into the technical check. A passing local fixture supports the stated calculation or rule. Claims about deployment, production outcomes and commercial return require separate evidence.

## How to run the participant check

Start with one operator and one prospective sponsor for a case. Give each a realistic task from the final column, without naming the control to use. Record the visitor’s initial prediction, the record they inspect, the action taken, their explanation of the consequence and any help they need. Then change one fact and ask them to repeat the decision. For the sponsor, change a cost or workload assumption and ask what evidence would reverse the funding recommendation. Observe whether visitors explore another path voluntarily. Completing the task, enjoying it and explaining it correctly are distinct observations. [GOV.UK guidance on moderated usability testing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing)
